About Five Seasons Radon

8+ years of protecting Iowa families from dangerous radon gas. Licensed, insured, and committed to your safety.

Who We Are

Five Seasons Radon is a locally owned and operated radon mitigation company serving Cedar Rapids, Linn County, and surrounding Iowa communities. With over 8 years of hands-on experience, we specialize in testing and eliminating dangerous radon gas from homes and businesses throughout Eastern Iowa.

We got into this business for one reason: to protect families. Iowa consistently ranks among the top states for radon exposure, and Cedar Rapids is no exception. Linn County homes frequently test above the EPA action level of 4 pCi/L, putting families at risk every single day they go without mitigation.

Our team of certified radon professionals uses proven, industry-standard techniques — primarily sub-slab depressurization systems — to reduce radon levels quickly and effectively. Every installation is backed by our guarantee to bring your radon levels below 4 pCi/L, and we include post-mitigation testing to verify results.

Cedar Rapids' Radon Mitigation Specialists

Five Seasons Radon was founded to address one of the most overlooked health risks in Linn County homes: radon gas. Iowa consistently ranks among the highest radon states in the nation, with the IDPH estimating that roughly 70% of Iowa homes have elevated radon levels. Cedar Rapids and the surrounding communities sit on geological formations that allow radon to seep into homes at concentrations that, over time, create serious health risks.

Our team holds NEHA-NRPP certification — the nationally recognized standard for radon professionals — and we carry all required Iowa licensing and insurance. Every mitigation system we install follows EPA best practices and is designed for the specific construction type and soil conditions of your home. We don't use one-size-fits-all solutions; we engineer each system to achieve the lowest radon levels possible.

We serve homeowners, real estate transactions, and property managers throughout the Cedar Rapids metro area and surrounding communities. If you've tested your home and found elevated radon, or if you're buying or selling a home where radon is a concern, we're the local experts you can trust for fast, reliable, and code-compliant mitigation.
